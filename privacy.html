Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AdenTweet processes information when you create an account, use social features, send messages, publish content, or contact support. AdenTweet is an Arabic-first social conversation service. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to qtbm.dev@gmail.com.

Information we process

We process the account information you provide, including display name, username, email address, password-derived authentication record, profile details, and settings. We process content you submit, such as posts, replies, media, direct messages, anonymous Sarahni messages, reports, verification requests, and support communications. We also process service information necessary for operation and safety, such as session identifiers, device or application information, access timestamps, network/security logs, content interaction records, and post view events.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts; provide feeds, profiles, messaging, communities, anonymous-message controls, notifications, verification, and moderation; prevent spam, abuse, fraud, and security incidents; comply with applicable law; and improve reliability. A post-view event is used to provide an aggregate view count. A Sarahni sender is not shown to the recipient; limited service-side anti-abuse records may be retained to enforce rate limits and investigate misuse.

Visibility and sharing

Public-profile information and content you choose to publish may be visible to other users and may be copied or shared by them. Direct messages and Sarahni messages are available to their intended recipients, subject to our safety and legal obligations. We may disclose information to hosting, storage, security, communications, and professional service providers that process it under instructions, or where required to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or address abuse. We do not sell personal information for money.

Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, enforce policies, meet legal obligations, and protect safety. You may request account and associated-data deletion through the process on the Data Deletion page. Some records may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or backup integrity.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. No transmission or storage method is completely secure; please protect your password and do not share account credentials.

Your choices

You can update profile and privacy settings in the application, including whether to accept anonymous Sarahni messages. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection by contacting us. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Children

AdenTweet is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required in the user’s jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to this policy, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes

We may update this Policy to reflect service or legal changes. We will publish the revised version and update its effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app.
