Account and Data Deletion

Effective date: August 18, 2026

You may request deletion of your AdenTweet account and associated personal data by emailing m775371829@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. Use the subject line “AdenTweet Data Deletion Request” and include your username and the email associated with the account.

What happens next

We will verify the request, confirm the account identity, and begin deletion or de-identification of account profile information, local account credentials, public content under our control, and service records that are no longer needed. We aim to respond within 30 days, subject to local law and request complexity.

What may be retained

We may retain limited records where needed for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal obligations, enforcement of our terms, or backup integrity. Content already shared, quoted, reposted, copied, or exported by other users may remain outside our direct control. Anonymous-message anti-abuse records may be retained only as needed for safety and compliance.

Alternative requests

You may also request correction of inaccurate account information or ask questions about retention by contacting the same address. See the Privacy Policy for more detail.
